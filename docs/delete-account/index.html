Delete your Qistly account

App: Qistly (com.qistly.app) · Last updated: 22 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your Qistly account and the data linked to it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to shahabmustafa18@gmail.com with the subject "Qistly account deletion request".
  2. Write the email address you use to sign in to Qistly and your shop / branch name, so we can verify the account.
  3. We will confirm by email and delete the account within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email, login and role).
  • Customer and guarantor details, CNIC numbers and CNIC photos stored by your shop, if you ask for them to be deleted.
  • Installment plans, payment records and notes linked to the deleted data.

What may be kept

  • Audit-log entries (who changed what and when) may be kept in anonymised form for up to 90 days for security and fraud prevention, then removed.
  • Backups are overwritten automatically within about 30 days.
  • Anything we are required by law to keep.

See also our Privacy Policy.
