Privacy Policy

App: Qistly (com.qistly.app) · Last updated: 22 November 2026

Qistly is an installment (qist) management app used by shop owners and their staff to record customers, installment plans, payments and expenses. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it is handled. The app is intended for business use by shop owners and authorised staff, not for children.

1. Who uses the app

Two groups of people have data in Qistly: (a) staff and owners who sign in to the app, and (b) customers and guarantors of the shop, whose details are entered into the app by the shop. Customers and guarantors do not use the app themselves; the shop that enters their data is responsible for having their permission to do so.

2. Data we collect

  • Account data (staff / owners): name, email address and password (or Google account sign-in), role and branch.
  • Customer and guarantor data entered by the shop: name, phone number, address, CNIC number, installment plan details, payment history, and notes.
  • Photos / documents: pictures of CNIC (front and back) of customers and guarantors, taken with the camera or chosen from the gallery, when the user adds them.
  • Business records: installment plans, payments, investments, expenses and an audit log of who changed what and when.
  • Device preferences: theme choice (light/dark) saved on the device.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, SMS, microphone recordings or advertising identifiers.

3. How we use the data

  • To provide the app: sign-in, showing customers, plans, dues and reports.
  • To read CNIC photos and auto-fill the form (see section 4).
  • To keep an audit trail and protect the shop's records from misuse.
  • To fix bugs and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Services that process data

  • Supabase – our backend. Account data, business records and uploaded document photos are stored in a Supabase database and file storage, protected by access rules so that each shop can see only its own data.
  • Google Sign-In – if you choose "Sign in with Google", Google shares your name and email with us to create your account.
  • CNIC text reading – when you ask the app to read a CNIC photo, the image may be processed on-device (Google ML Kit text recognition) and/or sent securely to our server function, which may use an AI service to extract the text. The result is used only to fill the form.

These providers process data only to provide their service to us.

5. Data sharing

Data is visible only to the owner and staff of the same shop, according to their role and branch. We do not share data with third parties except the service providers listed above, or if required by law.

6. Security

Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections and stored on access-controlled servers. Access is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

7. Data retention and deletion

Business records are kept as long as the shop's account is active, because installment history must be preserved. You can ask us to delete your account or specific data (for example a customer's CNIC photos) by contacting us below. We will delete it unless we must keep it for legal reasons.

8. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. Customers and guarantors can make the request through the shop that holds their data or directly to us.

9. Children

Qistly is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
